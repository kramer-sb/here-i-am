# The Garage Door Incident

**Date:** 2026-08-21

In what was a comedy of errors, I have had to learn how to program my garage door opener remotes and app. TL DR; I succeed in doing this today! I actually printed info from the website bc watching a video help lead to this... mistake. 

Someone got into the Jeep and didn't shut the hatch well enough, but the garage door shut anyway; it would, because it was pushing down. I went to leave later, and on the way up, the the hatch caught on a hinge and the beeps began, loudly. Had to fiddle with it and manually open the door with that handy red rope. Ran the errand, came back to address snapping it back together. 

I'd switched internet providers recently, severing the connection between the app and the door, so I thought I'd go ahead and knock that out while I'm here. 

After that, it's a blur. Buttons were pushed. Lights flashed. Beeps rang out. The door came crashing down and one side fell out of its track. I was undone for a minute, ngl. I have, more than a few times especially this year, shouted at no one in partcular, "I am tired of the struggle!" But, I probably phrased it more like Samuel Jackson did on the plane. 🤬🤭 Again, blur.

I was unable to get anyone here that night, so I did not sleep soundly, with the door being open enough that someone could slip under. It COULD happen, even in my little spot of suburbia. It didn't. 😅

Next day, someone came out, and fixed it in record time. 🙏🏻

That was Tuesday afternoon. All this time, I've been using the button on the wall. Open the door. Move the Jeep to the driveway. Get out of the Jeep and push the button on the wall. Go out the front door & lock it. Get in Jeep. Leave, reverse the process. Tiresome is putting it nicely. 

But today I fixed it. I am a badass.
